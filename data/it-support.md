# Starriver Tech IT Support Guide

## IT Service Desk

The IT service desk helps you with hardware, software, accounts, and network problems. It is open from 08:30 to 19:00 on working days.
Open a ticket in the IT service portal, or send a message to the #it-help channel in Slack. For an urgent problem, call extension 1999.

## Standard Laptops

Engineers get a MacBook Pro with 32 GB of memory. Other employees get a MacBook Air or a Windows laptop.
If your job needs a different laptop, your manager must explain the reason in the ticket.

## Laptop Replacement

The company replaces laptops every 4 years. IT sends you a message when your laptop is due for replacement.
If your laptop is broken, IT repairs it or gives you a loan laptop on the same day.
If you break your laptop because you were careless, for example if you drop it, the company can ask you to pay part of the repair cost.

## Accessories

You can request one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, and a headset. Request them in the IT service portal.
You can take a monitor home for remote work. Register it in the IT service portal first.

## Shared Drives

Save work files in Google Drive shared drives, not in your personal "My Drive". When you leave the company, the company deletes your "My Drive" after 30 days.
Each team has its own shared drive. To get access to another team's drive, ask the owner of that drive.

## Video Conferencing

Use Google Meet for internal meetings. Use Zoom only when a customer asks for it.
Each meeting room has a camera and a speaker. If the meeting room equipment does not work, call extension 1999.

## VPN

Connect through the company VPN when you work outside the office. Use the GlobalProtect app on your laptop.
If the VPN does not connect, restart the app first. If it still does not work, open a ticket.

## Email Rules

Each mailbox has 50 GB of storage. The system keeps emails for 7 years, also after you delete them.
Do not forward company email automatically to a personal email address. The system blocks automatic forwarding to outside addresses.

## Approved AI Tools

The company gives all employees access to an internal AI assistant. It runs on the company cloud account, and it does not use our data for training.
Engineers can also use the approved AI coding assistant in their code editor. Request a license in the IT service portal.
For other AI tools, open a ticket. The Security team reviews the tool before you use it.

## Password Reset

If you forget your password, reset it at the self-service password page with your MFA device.
If you also lost your MFA device, call the IT service desk. IT checks your identity with a video call before it resets your account.
