# Starriver Tech Workplace Health and Safety Policy

## Emergency Evacuation

When the fire alarm sounds, leave the building at once by the nearest emergency stairs. Do not use the elevators.
The assembly point is the park across the road from the main entrance. Each floor has a fire warden who checks that everyone left the floor.
The company does an evacuation drill two times each year.

## Earthquakes

During an earthquake, get under a desk and hold on to it. Stay away from windows. Do not run out of the building while it shakes.
After the shaking stops, follow the instructions of the fire wardens. Leave the building only if the wardens tell you to.

## Fire Safety

Do not block emergency exits or fire doors. Do not use personal electric heaters or extension cords that are not approved.
Each floor has fire extinguishers next to the elevators. Learn where they are on your first day.

## First Aid

Each floor has a first aid kit in the pantry. The names of the trained first aid persons are on the notice board next to the kit.
An automated external defibrillator (AED) is at the reception on floor 12.
For a medical emergency, call 119 first. Then tell the reception.

## Workplace Injuries

If you are injured at work or on your way to or from work, report it to your manager and HR within 24 hours.
HR helps you with the occupational injury claim. During occupational injury leave, the company pays your full salary.

## Ergonomics

Each desk has an adjustable chair and a monitor arm. You can request a standing desk, a footrest, or a vertical mouse from Facilities.
An occupational health nurse can check your workstation. Book a check in the Facilities system.

## Typhoon Days

When the local government announces that offices close because of a typhoon, the office is closed and you do not have to work. The company still pays your salary for that day.
If you want to work, work from home. The company does not count work on a typhoon day as overtime unless your manager asked you to work.
If only your home area closes, and the office area does not, you can take the day off. It does not count as leave.

## Infectious Diseases

If you have a fever or symptoms of an infectious disease, do not come to the office. Tell your manager and work from home or take sick leave.
If you have a confirmed infectious disease, such as influenza or COVID-19, tell HR. HR keeps your information confidential.

## Mental Health

Talk to your manager or HR if your workload is too high or if you feel stress for a long time.
You can use the free counseling of the Employee Assistance Program. You can also book a talk with the occupational health nurse.

## Occupational Health Nurse

An occupational health nurse is in the head office every Tuesday and Thursday from 10:00 to 16:00.
The nurse gives health advice, checks blood pressure, and helps employees who return to work after a long illness. Book a time in the Facilities system.
