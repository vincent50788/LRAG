# Starriver Tech Compensation and Payroll Policy

## Pay Day

The company pays salaries on the 5th day of each month for the previous month. If the 5th day is a holiday, the company pays on the working day before it.
The company pays salaries only to a bank account in your name. Give your bank account to HR in the onboarding form. To change the account, submit the change before the 25th day of the month.

## Salary Structure

Your monthly salary has three parts: the base salary, the meal allowance, and job allowances. Job allowances include the on-call allowance and the team lead allowance.
Your offer letter shows the annual salary. The annual salary is 14 months of the monthly salary. The 2 extra months are the guaranteed year-end bonus.

## Salary Review

The company reviews salaries once each year in March. The review uses your performance rating, the market salary data, and the company results.
Salary increases start on April 1. A promotion can also give a salary increase at the time of the promotion.
Do not discuss the salary review result with your manager before the official letter. The letter comes from HR.

## Year-End Bonus

The guaranteed year-end bonus is 2 months of base salary. The company pays it before the Lunar New Year.
If you joined during the year, you get a part of the bonus for the number of full months that you worked.
If you resign before the pay day of the bonus, you still get the part for the months that you worked.

## Performance Bonus

The company can pay a performance bonus in addition to the guaranteed year-end bonus. The amount depends on the company profit and your performance rating.
The company pays the performance bonus in April. To get it, you must be an employee on the day of payment. Employees on a performance improvement plan do not get a performance bonus.

## Payslips

You can see your payslip in the HR system on pay day. The payslip shows each part of your salary, the overtime pay, and each deduction.
The company does not print payslips. You can download a PDF copy for a bank loan or a visa application.

## Income Tax Withholding

The company withholds income tax from your salary each month according to the law. In January, the company gives you the annual withholding statement for your tax return.
If you want a different withholding rate, send the form to the Payroll team.

## Labor Pension

Each month, the company pays 6% of your salary into your personal labor pension account. You can also add a voluntary contribution of up to 6% of your salary. Your voluntary contribution reduces your taxable income.
Change your voluntary contribution in the HR system. The change starts in the next month.

## Payroll Errors

If you think that your salary is wrong, tell the Payroll team within 30 days after pay day. Give the payslip and an explanation.
If the company paid too little, it pays the difference on the next pay day. If the company paid too much, the Payroll team agrees with you on how to pay it back.

## Salary Advance

In an emergency, you can request a salary advance of up to 1 month of base salary. The HR director must approve it.
The company deducts the advance from your salary in equal amounts over a maximum of 6 months.
