# Starriver Tech Recruitment and Referral Policy

## Job Openings

All open jobs are on the careers page and in the HR system. To open a new job, the hiring manager submits a headcount request. The department vice president and the HR director must approve it.
HR posts the job inside the company for 5 working days before it posts the job outside the company.

## Internal Transfers

After 12 months in your current role, you can apply for an internal job. Tell your current manager before you apply.
If you get the new job, your current manager agrees with the new manager on your start date. The transfer usually happens within 2 months.
Your salary and your seniority stay the same, unless the new job is at a different grade.

## Employee Referral Bonus

If you refer a candidate and the company hires that person, you get a referral bonus:
- NT$30,000 for engineering jobs.
- NT$15,000 for other jobs.
The company pays the bonus after the new employee completes probation. Submit referrals in the HR system. Managers do not get a bonus for referrals to their own team.

## Interview Process

Most jobs have three steps: a phone screen with HR, a technical or skill interview, and a final interview with the hiring manager.
Engineering jobs also include a take-home exercise or a live coding interview.
Give feedback in the recruitment system within 24 hours after each interview.

## Interviewer Training

Before you interview candidates, complete the interviewer training in the learning system. The training explains fair questions and how to score candidates.
Do not ask about age, marriage, pregnancy, religion, or political opinions. These questions are not permitted.

## Offers

HR makes all job offers. Do not tell a candidate about the salary or an offer before HR does.
An offer is valid for 7 days. The offer letter gives the job title, the salary, the start date, and the probation period.

## Background Checks

For some jobs, such as finance and security jobs, the company does a background check before the start date. The check includes education and past employment.
The company asks for the written consent of the candidate first.

## Interns

The company hires interns for summer internships (July to August) and for part-time internships during the school year.
Interns get an hourly wage that is above the legal minimum wage. Interns can work a maximum of 20 hours each week during the school year.

## Rehiring Former Employees

Former employees who left the company on good terms can apply again. If you return within 1 year, your old years of service count for your annual leave.
Employees whom the company dismissed for misconduct cannot work for the company again.

## Relatives at Work

Relatives of employees can work at the company. Tell HR if a relative applies.
A person cannot manage a relative, approve the expenses of a relative, or take part in the hiring decision for a relative.
