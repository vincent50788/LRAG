1. Booking and Approval

Every overseas trip needs a travel request form. Your department manager and
your department vice president must both approve it before you book anything.

Book flights through the company travel desk, Skyline Travel, at least 14 days
before departure. A booking made later needs a written reason from your
manager.

Fly economy class. For a flight of 6 hours or more, you may use premium
economy. Business class is not allowed, unless the CEO approves it in writing.

Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months after the day you return. You
are responsible for your visa. The company pays the visa fee, but it does not pay
for a passport.

2. Hotels and Daily Allowance

The hotel limit for each night depends on the region. Asia: NT$4,500. Europe and
North America: NT$7,000. All other regions: NT$5,500. The company pays only
for the room. Laundry and the minibar are your own cost.

The daily meal allowance is NT$1,200 in Asia, NT$1,800 in Europe, and NT$2,000
in North America. You do not need receipts for the allowance. If your hotel
includes breakfast, subtract NT$300 from the allowance for that day.

Use public transport or the airport train first. A taxi is allowed from the airport to
the hotel when you carry a large bag or arrive after 22:00.

3. Insurance and Emergencies

The company buys travel insurance for every overseas trip. You do not need to
apply. It pays up to NT$5,000,000 for an accident and up to NT$300,000 for
medical costs. Keep every medical receipt.

In an emergency, call the Starriver Travel Hotline at +886-2-5550-0199. It works
24 hours every day.

lf you lose your passport, report it to the local police first. Then contact the
Taiwan representative office in that country, and call HR within 24 hours. If your
flight is cancelled, call Skyline Travel. The company pays the rebooking fee.

4. Cash Advance and Coming Home

You may ask for a cash advance of up to NT$30,000 for an overseas trip. Send
the request to the Finance Department at least 5 working days before you leave.
Return any unused cash within 5 days after you come back.

Write a short trip report within 7 days after you return. Send it to your manager
and to the Finance Department.

After a flight of 10 hours or more, you may start work at 13:00 on the next
working day. You do not need to take leave for this.
