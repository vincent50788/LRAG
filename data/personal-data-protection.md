# Starriver Tech Personal Data Protection Policy

This policy follows the Personal Data Protection Act of Taiwan.

## Employee Personal Data

HR keeps the personal data of employees, such as ID numbers, bank accounts, addresses, and health information. Only HR and Payroll staff can see this data.
You can see and correct your own data in the HR system. If something is wrong that you cannot change, tell HR.

## Customer Personal Data

Use customer personal data only for the purpose that the customer agreed to. Do not copy customer data to test systems. Use masked data or fake data for tests.
Get access to customer data only if your job needs it. Your manager approves the access, and the Security team reviews it every 6 months.

## Data Retention

The company keeps employee records for 5 years after an employee leaves. After that, the company deletes or destroys them.
The company keeps customer data only for the contract period and the legal retention period. Each team must delete data that it no longer needs.

## Data Subject Requests

A customer or an employee can ask to see, correct, or delete their personal data. Send all such requests to privacy@starriver.example.
The Privacy team must reply within 15 days. Do not reply to these requests yourself.

## Personal Data Breaches

A personal data breach is when personal data goes to a person who must not see it, for example an email sent to the wrong customer.
Report a possible breach to the Privacy team at once, and within 24 hours at the latest. The Privacy team decides if the company must tell the customers and the authorities.
Do not try to investigate or fix the breach alone.

## CCTV

The company uses cameras at the office entrances, the reception, and the server room. There are no cameras in work areas, toilets, or the lactation rooms.
The company keeps the video for 30 days. Only Facilities and Security can see it.

## Monitoring of Company Systems

The company logs the use of company systems, such as sign-ins, VPN connections, and file downloads, for security.
The company does not read your emails or chat messages, except in an investigation that Legal approves.

## Vendors that Process Personal Data

Before a vendor processes personal data for the company, the vendor must sign a data processing agreement. The Legal Department has a template.
The Security team checks the security of the vendor before the contract.
