# Starriver Tech Employee Benefits

These benefits apply to all full-time employees after their first day of work, unless a section gives a different rule.

## Labor and National Health Insurance

The company enrolls you in Labor Insurance, Employment Insurance, and National Health Insurance on your first day. The company pays its legal share of the premiums. Your share comes out of your monthly salary.
You can add your spouse, children, and parents as dependents on your National Health Insurance. Send the dependent form and a copy of the household registration to HR.

## Group Insurance

The company buys group insurance for each employee at no cost to the employee. It includes life insurance of NT$2,000,000, accident insurance of NT$3,000,000, and hospital insurance.
You can add family members to the group hospital insurance at a discounted price. You pay the premium for family members. Make changes in November each year, or within 30 days after a marriage or the birth of a child.
To make a claim, send the receipt and the medical certificate to the insurance broker. The contact details are on the HR intranet page.

## Annual Health Check

Each year, the company pays for one health check at a partner hospital. Employees under 40 get the standard package. Employees aged 40 and above get the advanced package, which adds an abdominal ultrasound.
Book the health check in the HR system. You can take a half day of paid official leave for the health check.

## Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) gives free, confidential counseling. Each employee can use 6 sessions each year. The sessions cover mental health, legal questions, financial questions, and family problems.
Call the EAP hotline or book online. The company does not receive the names of employees who use the EAP.

## Meal Allowance

Each employee gets a meal allowance of NT$2,400 each month. It is part of your monthly salary and is tax-free up to the legal limit.
The company does not give a meal allowance for days of unpaid leave.

## Festival and Birthday Gifts

The company gives a gift voucher of NT$1,000 for each of these festivals: Dragon Boat Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, and Lunar New Year.
In your birthday month, you get a birthday voucher of NT$800 and a half day of paid birthday leave. Use the birthday leave in your birthday month. You cannot keep it for later.

## Employee Travel Subsidy

Each year, the Employee Welfare Committee pays a travel subsidy of up to NT$8,000 for each employee. You can use it for the company trip or for your own trip.
For your own trip, send the receipts for hotels, flights, or travel packages to the Welfare Committee before December 10. Receipts must be from the same calendar year.

## Sports and Fitness Subsidy

The company pays 50% of a gym or sports club membership, up to NT$6,000 each year. Send the receipt to the Welfare Committee each quarter.
The company also pays for sports clubs of employees, such as badminton, running, and basketball. A club needs at least 5 members to get a budget.

## Wedding, Childbirth, and Funeral Payments

The Welfare Committee gives a cash gift of NT$6,000 when an employee marries, and NT$6,000 for the birth of each child.
When a family member of an employee dies, the Welfare Committee gives a condolence payment of NT$5,000 to NT$10,000. The amount depends on the relationship.
Send the request and a copy of the certificate (marriage, birth, or death) within 3 months.

## Employee Stock Ownership Plan

Employees with at least 1 year of service can join the Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). Each month, you can buy company shares with 1% to 10% of your base salary. The company adds 20% of your contribution.
If you leave the company within 2 years after you join, you lose the company contribution. You keep the shares that you paid for.
