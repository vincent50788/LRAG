# Starriver Tech Family and Parental Leave Policy

This policy follows the Act of Gender Equality in Employment. The company pays some types of leave above the legal minimum.

## Maternity Leave

A female employee who gives birth gets 8 weeks of maternity leave with full pay. You can start the leave before the birth.
For a miscarriage, you get 1 to 4 weeks of leave. The number of weeks depends on the month of the pregnancy.
Request maternity leave in the HR system and attach the birth certificate or the medical certificate.

## Paternity Leave

An employee whose spouse gives birth gets 8 days of paid paternity leave. You can use the days in the period from 15 days before the birth to 15 days after the birth.
You can take the days separately. You do not have to take them all together.

## Prenatal Checkup Leave

A pregnant employee gets 7 days of paid prenatal checkup leave. An employee whose spouse is pregnant gets 7 days of paid leave to go with the spouse to checkups.
You can take this leave in half days or in hours.

## Parental Leave Without Pay

After 6 months of service, you can request parental leave without pay for each child under 3 years old. The maximum is 2 years for each child.
During this leave, you can keep your Labor Insurance and National Health Insurance. The government can pay a parental leave allowance.
Request the leave at least 10 days before it starts.

## Family Care Leave

You can use up to 7 days each year to care for a family member who is ill, or for important family events. This leave is unpaid. These days count as part of your personal leave.
The company does not reduce your attendance bonus or your performance rating because of family care leave.

## Breastfeeding and Lactation Room

An employee who breastfeeds a child under 2 years old gets 60 minutes each day for breastfeeding or for pumping milk. This time counts as working time.
Each office has a lactation room with a refrigerator. Book the room in the meeting room system.

## Childcare Subsidy

The company pays a childcare subsidy of NT$3,000 each month for each child under 6 years old.
Send a copy of the birth certificate to HR. The subsidy starts in the month after HR receives the documents.

## Reduced Hours for Parents

An employee with a child under 3 years old can ask to work 1 hour less each day. The company reduces your salary for the reduced hours.
You can also ask to change your working hours, for example 08:00–17:00, to take your child to school.

## Menstrual Leave

A female employee who has difficulty working during menstruation can take 1 day of menstrual leave each month.
The first 3 days each year do not count as sick leave. Days after that count as sick leave. The company pays half of your salary for menstrual leave.

## Returning to Work

When you return from maternity leave or parental leave, you return to your old job, or to a job of the same level and salary.
Meet with your manager and HR in your first week back to agree on your work plan.
