# Starriver Tech New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Your First Day

About 1 week before your first day, HR sends you an email with the onboarding form. Complete the form before your first day. It asks for your bank account, your emergency contact, and your address.
Your manager gets a ticket to prepare your laptop and your accounts.

## Documents to Bring

On your first day, bring these documents:
- Your national ID card (or your Alien Resident Certificate and work permit).
- Your bank passbook.
- Your highest degree certificate.
- The resignation certificate from your last employer, if you had one.
- A health check report from the last 3 months.

## First Day Schedule

Arrive at the reception on floor 12 at 09:30. HR meets you and gives you your access card.
In the morning, HR explains the handbook and the HR system. At lunch, your team takes you out to eat. The company pays for this lunch. In the afternoon, IT gives you your laptop and helps you sign in.

## Laptop and Account Setup

IT gives you a laptop that is ready to use. On the first day, you sign in to your Google Workspace account, Slack, and the HR system.
Set up multi-factor authentication on your first day. You cannot use company systems without it after the first 24 hours.
Your manager requests access to the other systems that your job needs.

## Probation Period

The probation period is 3 months. During probation, your manager meets with you every 2 weeks.
At the end of probation, your manager completes a probation review in the HR system. If the review is positive, your employment continues. If it is not positive, the company can extend probation one time by up to 3 months.

## Buddy Program

Each new employee gets a buddy from a different team. Your buddy is a colleague with at least 1 year of service. Your buddy answers your questions about the company culture and daily work.
Meet your buddy at least one time each week during your first month. The company pays NT$500 each month for one coffee or lunch with your buddy during the first 3 months.

## Mandatory Training

Complete these online courses in the learning system within 30 days after your first day:
- Information security basics.
- Personal data protection.
- Prevention of sexual harassment.
- Workplace health and safety.
Your manager can see if you completed them.

## First 90 Days

In your first week, agree on a 30-60-90 day plan with your manager. The plan lists what you learn and what you deliver in each period.
At day 90, you and your manager review the plan. This review is a part of your probation review.
