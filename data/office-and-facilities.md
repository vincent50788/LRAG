# Starriver Tech Office and Facilities Guide

## Office Locations and Opening Hours

The head office is on floors 11 and 12 of the Starriver Building in Taipei. The Taichung office is on floor 8 of the Central Tower.
The offices are open from 07:00 to 22:00 on working days. To work outside these hours or on weekends, send a request to Facilities 1 working day before.

## Access Cards

Your access card opens the office doors and the elevators. Wear your access card where people can see it.
If you forget your access card, get a temporary card from the reception. Return it on the same day.
If you lose your access card, report it to Facilities. A new card costs NT$200.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the visitor system at least 1 hour before they arrive. Meet your visitor at the reception.
Visitors must wear a visitor badge and stay with an employee at all times. Visitors cannot enter the server room.

## Meeting Rooms

Book meeting rooms in Google Calendar. You can book a room for a maximum of 3 hours at one time.
If you do not arrive within 15 minutes after the start of your booking, the system releases the room.
Clean the whiteboard and take your things when you leave.

## Hot Desks and Lockers

Some teams use hot desks. Book a hot desk in the desk booking app before 09:00 on the same day.
Each employee who uses hot desks gets a personal locker. At the end of each day, put your things in your locker. Do not leave things on a hot desk overnight.

## Pantry

Each floor has a pantry with free coffee, tea, and snacks. The company refills the snacks on Monday and Thursday.
Put a label with your name and the date on food in the refrigerator. Every Friday at 17:00, the cleaners remove all food from the refrigerator.

## Printing

Print with your access card at any printer. The print job stays in the queue for 24 hours.
Print in black and white on both sides by default. For more than 100 color pages, ask Facilities first.
Put confidential paper documents in the locked shredding bins. Do not put them in the normal recycling bins.

## Mail and Deliveries

The reception receives packages and mail for employees. When a package arrives, the reception sends you a Slack message.
Collect your package within 3 working days. You can receive small personal packages at the office. Do not use the office address for large personal deliveries, such as furniture.

## Clean Desk Rule

At the end of the day, do not leave confidential documents on your desk. Lock your drawers.
The cleaners clean the desks every Wednesday evening. On Wednesday, keep your desk clear.

## Facility Repairs

If something in the office is broken, such as a light, the air conditioning, or a chair, open a ticket in the Facilities system.
Facilities usually fixes problems within 2 working days. For an urgent problem, such as a water leak, call the reception.

## Pets and Children

Pets are not permitted in the office, except assistance dogs.
In an emergency, you can bring your child to the office for one day. Tell your manager first. Your child must stay with you at all times.
