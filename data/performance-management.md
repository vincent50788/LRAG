# Starriver Tech Performance Management Guide

## Review Cycle

The company does performance reviews two times each year. The mid-year review is in July. The annual review is in January.
Only the annual review affects your salary review and your performance bonus. The mid-year review is for feedback and goal changes.
Employees who joined less than 3 months before a review do not get a rating in that review.

## Goal Setting

At the start of each half year, set 3 to 5 goals with your manager. Use the OKR format: one objective and two to four measurable key results.
Record your goals in the performance system within the first 3 weeks of the half year. You can change goals during the half year if your manager agrees.

## Self-Assessment

Before each review, write a self-assessment in the performance system. For each goal, describe your results and give evidence, such as links to documents or data.
The self-assessment is due 2 weeks after the start of the review period.

## Peer Feedback

For the annual review, choose 3 to 5 colleagues to give feedback about you. Your manager can add more people.
Feedback is not anonymous to your manager. Your manager shares a summary of the feedback with you, but not the names of the persons who gave it.

## Rating Scale

The company uses a 5-level rating scale:
- 5: Far above expectations.
- 4: Above expectations.
- 3: Meets expectations.
- 2: Partly meets expectations.
- 1: Does not meet expectations.
Most employees get a rating of 3.

## Calibration

After managers write their assessments, the department leaders meet to compare the ratings. This meeting is calibration. Calibration makes sure that teams use the same standard.
Calibration can change your rating. Your manager tells you your final rating after calibration.

## Performance Improvement Plan

If your rating is 1, or if your rating is 2 two times in a row, your manager makes a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with you.
A PIP lasts 60 days. It has clear goals and a weekly meeting with your manager. HR joins the first and the last meeting.
If you do not meet the PIP goals, the company can move you to a different role or end your employment according to the law.

## Promotion

The company considers promotions once each year, after the annual review. Your manager nominates you. A promotion committee makes the decision.
To get a promotion, you usually need a rating of 4 or 5 in the last annual review, and you must already work at the level of the next job grade.
The promotion starts on April 1 together with the salary review.

## Rating Appeals

If you do not agree with your final rating, you can appeal within 10 working days after you receive it. Send the appeal to HR with your reasons and evidence.
An HR business partner and a manager from a different department review the appeal. They reply within 20 working days. Their decision is final.
