# Starriver Tech Learning and Development Policy

## Training Budget

Each employee has a personal training budget of NT$20,000 each year. You can use it for courses, certification exams, conferences, and books.
Unused budget does not move to the next year. Your manager must approve each use of the budget before you pay.

## External Courses

To take a paid external course, submit a training request in the HR system. Give the course name, the cost, the dates, and how the course helps your work.
After the course, submit the receipt and the completion certificate within 30 days. The company then pays the cost back to you.
If the course is during working hours, you do not need to take leave.

## Certification Exams

The company pays the exam fee for certifications that are related to your job, such as cloud, security, or project management certifications.
The company pays for a maximum of 2 attempts for the same exam. If you pass, you get a one-time bonus of NT$5,000.

## Conferences

You can go to 1 conference each year with your training budget. For a conference outside Taiwan, your department vice president must also approve it.
Travel costs for a conference follow the Business Travel and Expenses rules in the employee handbook.
After the conference, give a short talk or write a summary for your team within 2 weeks.

## Internal Tech Talks

The engineering team holds a tech talk every second Thursday at 16:00. All employees can join.
If you give a tech talk, you get a gift voucher of NT$1,000. Sign up on the tech talk page in Confluence.

## Language Learning Subsidy

The company pays 70% of the cost of language courses for English, Japanese, and Korean, up to NT$12,000 each year. This subsidy is separate from your training budget.
You must attend at least 80% of the classes to get the subsidy.

## Book Subsidy

You can buy professional books and e-books with your training budget. You do not need approval for each book under NT$1,500.
Physical books that you buy with the budget belong to the company library after you read them.

## Mentorship Program

The mentorship program matches employees with a senior colleague from a different department for 6 months. Mentors and mentees meet at least one time each month.
Applications open in January and July. Apply on the HR intranet page.

## Study Leave

For a degree program that is related to your job, you can request up to 5 days of paid study leave each year for exams.
For a longer study program, you can request unpaid leave of up to 1 year. The department vice president and the HR director must approve it.

## Training Service Agreement

If the company pays more than NT$50,000 for one training program, you must sign a training service agreement.
The agreement asks you to stay with the company for a fixed time after the training, usually 1 year. If you resign earlier, you pay back a part of the training cost. The amount decreases each month that you stay.
