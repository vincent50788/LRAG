# Starriver Tech Procurement and Finance Policy

## Purchase Requests

To buy goods or services for the company, submit a purchase request in the finance system before you buy. Give the item, the vendor, the price, and the budget code.
Purchases without a purchase request are your own cost, except for small purchases with petty cash.

## Approval Limits

The approvals that a purchase needs depend on the total amount:
- Up to NT$10,000: your department manager.
- NT$10,001 to NT$100,000: your department manager and the Finance Department.
- NT$100,001 to NT$1,000,000: also your department vice president.
- More than NT$1,000,000: also the CEO.
Do not split one purchase into smaller purchases to avoid an approval.

## Vendor Selection

For a purchase above NT$100,000, get quotes from at least 3 vendors. If you cannot get 3 quotes, explain the reason in the purchase request.
A new vendor must complete the vendor registration form. The Finance Department checks the company registration and the bank account of the vendor.

## Company Credit Cards

Managers and employees who travel often can get a company credit card. The card limit is NT$100,000 each month.
Use the company card only for business costs. Do not take cash with the card.
Upload the receipts for all card payments to the expense system by the 5th day of the next month.

## Petty Cash

Each office has petty cash for small purchases of up to NT$3,000, such as office supplies or courier fees.
Get the money from the office administrator. Give the receipt to the administrator within 3 working days.

## Client Entertainment

You can invite customers or partners to a business meal. The limit is NT$2,000 for each person, including employees.
Before the meal, your manager must approve it. In the expense claim, write the names and the companies of all persons at the meal.
Alcohol is permitted only at client meals and only in reasonable amounts.

## Late-Night Meals and Taxis

If you work approved overtime until after 20:00, the company pays for dinner up to NT$200.
If you work until after 22:00, the company also pays for a taxi home. Attach the overtime approval to the expense claim.

## Invoice Rules

All receipts must be official Taiwan uniform invoices or official receipts. The invoice must show the company tax ID number, 89157312.
For e-invoices, give the company tax ID to the seller and choose the company carrier. For overseas costs, a normal receipt is sufficient.

## Budget Planning

Each department makes its budget for the next year in October. The Finance Department approves the budgets in December.
If a department needs more money during the year, its vice president sends a budget change request to the CFO.

## Fixed Assets

Equipment with a price of more than NT$80,000 and a life of more than 2 years is a fixed asset. Finance puts an asset tag on it.
Do not move, sell, or throw away fixed assets without approval from Finance. Each December, Finance checks all fixed assets.
