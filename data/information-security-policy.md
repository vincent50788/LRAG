# Starriver Tech Information Security Policy

## Accounts and Passwords

A company account password must have at least 12 characters. It must contain uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Change your password every 180 days.
Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all company systems. We recommend Google Authenticator or a hardware key from the company.
Do not share your account with other persons. Do not write passwords on sticky notes or in plain-text documents. We recommend that you use 1Password from the company to manage your passwords.

## Device Use

Turn on full-disk encryption on your company laptop. Keep the operating system updated. Lock the screen when you leave your desk (macOS: Control + Command + Q).
Do not install software on your company laptop without approval from the IT Department. For new software, open a ticket at the IT service desk. IT usually processes a ticket within 2 working days.
Personal devices (BYOD) can access only Slack and company email. Do not download customer data to a personal device.

## Data Classification

The company classifies data into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Top Secret.
Do not upload Confidential or Top Secret data to a personal cloud drive. Do not paste this data into external AI services, such as public chatbots. Use only internal AI tools that the company approves.
When you send Confidential data to an external party, use an encrypted archive. Give the password through a different channel, such as a phone call.

## Security Incident Reports

If you find a suspicious email, do not click its links or attachments. Use the Gmail "Report phishing" button, or forward the email to security@starriver.example.
If you lose a laptop or phone, report it to the IT service desk (extension 1999) within 1 hour. IT then locks the device remotely. If you think that another person uses your account, also report it to the IT service desk within 1 hour.
