# Starriver Tech Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Starriver Tech Co., Ltd. If this handbook conflicts with labor law, the law applies.

## Working Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 09:30–18:30. The lunch break is 12:30–13:30. The company uses flexible hours. You can arrive between 08:30 and 10:30. You can leave after 8 hours of work.
Record your arrival and departure with the company app "Starriver Clock". If you forget to record a time, submit a correction in the HR system within 3 working days. You can submit a maximum of 3 corrections each month.
An arrival after 10:30 counts as late. If you are late 3 times in one month, your manager meets with you.

## Leave

Annual leave follows the Labor Standards Act. After 6 months of service, you get 3 days. After 1 year, you get 7 days. After that, the number of days increases with your years of service. You can keep unused annual leave until June 30 of the next year. After that date, the company pays the unused days as wages.
For sick leave of up to 30 days in total each year, the company pays half of your salary. For sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days, attach a medical certificate.
Personal leave is unpaid. The maximum is 14 days each year.
Request leave in the HR system at least 1 working day before the leave. Your direct manager must approve it. For sudden sick leave, send a message to your manager before 10:00 on the same day. Then submit the request within 2 working days.
Marriage leave is 8 days. Bereavement leave is 3 to 8 days. The number of days depends on your relationship to the person who died. The company pays for both types of leave.

## Remote Work

After 3 months of employment, you can request a maximum of 2 days of remote work each week. Register the days in the HR system before the Friday of the previous week.
During remote work, stay online in Slack during working hours. Reply to messages within 30 minutes.
During remote work, do not use public Wi-Fi to access company systems. Always connect through the company VPN.

## Overtime

Request overtime in the HR system before you work it. Your manager must approve it. The company does not count overtime without approval.
On working days, the company pays the first 2 hours of overtime at 1.34 times the normal hourly wage. The company pays hours 3 and 4 at 1.67 times the normal hourly wage. Instead of overtime pay, you can choose compensatory time off. Use the compensatory time off within 6 months.
The maximum overtime is 46 hours each month.

## Business Travel and Expenses

Before a business trip, complete a travel request form. Your department manager must approve it. For an overseas trip, your department vice president must also approve it.
For domestic trips, the hotel limit is NT$2,500 per night. Different limits apply to overseas regions. The company pays the actual cost of High Speed Rail and Taiwan Railway tickets. Use a taxi only late at night (after 22:00) or when you carry large equipment.
Submit an expense claim within 30 days after the expense. Upload a photo of the original invoice or receipt to the expense system. The company does not accept late claims.
A single purchase above NT$10,000 needs approval from the Finance Department before the purchase.

## Code of Conduct

Employees must respect each other. The company prohibits all forms of discrimination, bullying, and sexual harassment.
If you experience or see improper behavior, report it to your direct manager or to HR. You can also send an email to the anonymous complaint mailbox, speakup@starriver.example. The company replies within 7 working days. The company keeps the identity of the person who reports confidential.
Do not accept gifts or entertainment from vendors with a value above NT$1,000. If you cannot refuse a gift, report it to the Legal Department within 3 days.
On external social media, do not disclose company information that is not public. Do not give personal opinions in the name of the company.

## Resignation

If you resign, submit your resignation before your last day:
- Less than 1 year of service: 10 days before your last day.
- At least 1 year and less than 3 years of service: 20 days before your last day.
- 3 years of service or more: 30 days before your last day.
On your last day, return your laptop, your access card, and all company property. Complete the offboarding checklist in the HR system.
